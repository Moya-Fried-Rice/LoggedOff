About
Logged Off is an NFC-integrated platform designed to connect the LPU-C Community with professional mental health support, provide crucial educational resources, and foster a comprehensive culture of digital responsibility.

Vision
To foster an inclusive and supportive LPU-Cavite community where innovative digital solutions promote mental health awareness, holistic well-being, and accessible support for every individual.

Mission
To create a centralized and accessible mental health platform for the LPU-Cavite community through meaningful design and NFC technology, providing a safe and supportive digital space for all.

Why LoggedOff?
Our main goal is to provide users with reliable information and resources to help them better understand and manage their mental well-being.

LoggedOff provides a centralized mental health platform catered to the needs of the entire LPU-Cavite Community. Whether you want to understand mental health, explore self-care practices, support someone you care about, or find professional help, you can start here.

NFC Keychain [Title]
One Tap Closer to Well-Being.

Access mental health resources with just one tap.

Why NFC?
Making mental health support more accessible.
NFC technology offers a fast, convenient, and contactless way to access mental health information and support. By integrating NFC into LoggedOff, we aim to reduce barriers to accessing reliable resources and encourage the LPU-Cavite community to prioritize mental well-being.

Your gateway to mental health resources, anytime, anywhere.
The Logged Off NFC Keychain provides a convenient way to access our mental health platform anytime, anywhere.

How to Use
Tap. Connect. Access Support.

Carry - Attach your Logged Off keychain to your bag or lanyard for convenient access to mental health resources anytime.
Tap - Hold your keychain near the top of your screen for IOS or the back of your NFC-enabled smartphone for Android.
Access- Tap the notification that appears to open the Logged Off platform and explore mental health resources, self-help strategies, and our partner information.
Connect - Find and contact partnered mental health services, such as the LPU-Cavite Guidance and Testing Center or Exalt Psychological Clinic, for professional support.

Note: Your smartphone must support NFC. Most modern smartphones have built-in NFC, but you may need to enable it in your settings.

Take a Moment for Yourself [Title]
Small Step, Meaningful Changes

Taking care of your mental well-being doesn’t have to be complicated. Explore these six tips and start with what feels manageable for you.

6 Ways to Care for Your Mental Well-Being

01 — Talk it out with someone you trust
Try to share and connect with your family, friends, or a colleague.

02 — Be kind to yourself
Move your body every day, eat well, and get enough sleep.

03 — Do what makes you happy
Make time for enjoyable activities such as painting, cooking, exercise, and reading.

04 — Be gentle with how you cope
Don’t turn to harmful drugs, alcohol, unhealthy substances and hurt yourself.

05 — Take two minutes to notice the world around you
Pause and bring your attention to your surroundings with this grounding exercise.

06 - Take a pause, breathe, and free yourself from the moment.

Try this grounding exercise:
What are five things I can see?
What are four things I can hear?
What can I smell?
What does it feel like to touch my knees, or something else within reach? How does it feel underneath my fingers?

06 — Reaching out for help is okay
You don’t have to navigate difficult moments alone. Support is available when you need it.

Remember, you are not alone, it's okay to seek help.

Get to Know Your Mind, [Title]
Understand Yourself Better.
Learn about mental health, recognize changes in your thoughts, feelings, and behavior, and understand when it may be time to seek support.

What Is Mental Health?
Mental health is about how you think, feel, and handle everyday life. It helps you cope with stress, recognize your strengths, do your daily activities, and connect with the people around you.

When Should You Pay Attention?
You may notice changes in your:
Thoughts — How you think and understand situations.
Feelings — How you experience and manage emotions.
Behavior — How you act and respond to others.
These changes may cause emotional distress and make everyday activities, such as school, work, or maintaining relationships, more difficult.

What Is a Mental Health Condition?
A mental health condition is a broad term that includes different mental health challenges, from specific disorders to ongoing emotional struggles that may need extra care and support.

When Might You Need Support?
It’s more than just having a bad day. When these struggles continue and affect your daily life, it may be a sign that you need support.
Remember: Seeking support is a positive step toward taking care of your mental well-being.

Mental Health in the Philippines
Mental health matters. Understanding it is the first step toward seeking support.

Mental Health by the Numbers
12.5 million Filipinos (11.3 to 11.6 percent of the population) are estimated to live with a mental health condition. (Global Burden of Disease, reported 2023)

3.6 million Filipinos were estimated to have a mental, neurological, or substance use disorder in a 2020 WHO assessment, likely an undercount per the DOH. (DOH and WHO, 2020)

3,379 Filipinos died by suicide in 2024, up from 3,133 in 2023. (Philippine Statistics Authority, 2025)

Why It Matters
Mental health challenges can affect anyone. Early awareness, reliable information, and access to appropriate support can make a difference.
