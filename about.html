About

Logged Off is an NFC-integrated platform designed to connect the LPU-C Community with professional mental health support, provide crucial educational resources, and foster a comprehensive culture of digital responsibility.

Vision

To foster an inclusive and supportive LPU-Cavite community where innovative digital solutions promote mental health awareness, holistic well-being, and accessible support for every individual.

Mission

To create a centralized and accessible mental health platform for the LPU-Cavite community through meaningful design and NFC technology, providing a safe and supportive digital space for all.

Why LoggedOff?

Our main goal is to provide users with reliable information and resources to help them better understand and manage their mental well-being.

LoggedOff provides a centralized mental health platform catered to the needs of the entire LPU-Cavite Community. Whether you want to understand mental health, explore self-care practices, support someone you care about, or find professional help, you can start here.

Log off from negativity, log in to self-care.
