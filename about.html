OUR MISSION

Bridging the Gap Between Awareness and Action

LoggedOff is an innovative, NFC-integrated web platform developed to promote cyberbullying awareness, digital safety education, and accessible mental health support. Online interactions play a central role in students' daily lives, but negative experiences such as harassment, exclusion, and cyberbullying can carry serious emotional and psychological consequences.

The core purpose of the platform is to provide an approachable, student-centered gateway where individuals can learn how to protect themselves online, recognize harmful behavior, and immediately discover trusted pathways to help.

By connecting physical NFC accessories with a digital resource hub, LoggedOff transforms passive awareness into proactive self-care. The platform maintains close partnerships with professional care providers, including Exalt Psychological Clinic and Consultancy and the LPU-C Guidance and Testing Center, ensuring that assistance is always accessible when students need it most.

NFC TECHNOLOGY

The LoggedOff NFC Keychain

The LoggedOff NFC Keychain is a compact physical accessory embedded with a near-field communication tag. Designed to be carried on a backpack, key ring, or lanyard, it functions as a discreet, physical link to digital safety support.

By tapping the keychain against any modern NFC-capable smartphone, the user is instantly directed to the LoggedOff platform without needing to remember a URL or search online during a stressful moment.

  1. Carry Attach the durable LoggedOff keychain to your bag, keys, or lanyard for daily, portable reassurance.
  2. Tap Hold the keychain against the back of any NFC-enabled smartphone (iOS or Android).
  3. Access Instantly load the LoggedOff platform to review digital safety resources, incident coping strategies, or partner information.
  4. Connect Directly contact partnered mental health services such as LPU-C Guidance or Exalt Clinic for confidential help.

Note: NFC functionality requires an NFC-enabled smartphone. Most modern smartphones have NFC enabled natively.
